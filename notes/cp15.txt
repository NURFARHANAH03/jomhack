Aggregating:
"Taking a bunch of connected pieces and turning them into one single thing."
Think of it like this:
• In normal life: If I ask about your friends, you don't list every single person, their age, and their favorite food. You just say, "I have 5 friends." You took a big list of people and squished it into a single number. That is aggregation.
• In SQL queries: Instead of showing 20 rows of every single thing a customer bought, you show one row with the total amount they spent.
• In database design: Instead of just saying "a mechanic fixes a car," you treat that entire "car fix" event as one single job so you can attach a bill to it.
